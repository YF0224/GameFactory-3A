- note_burst_good: Softer round chime burst
- miss_thud: Dull dropped-mallet thud with a faint detune
- hold_complete: Clean sustained reed ring completing a hold note
- combo_click: Light brass click as combo tiers rise
- section_bell: Two-note turning bell as the song section changes
- finale_warn: Single deep anticipatory bell for the finale cue
- dual_pop: Twin simultaneous chime pop for a dual note
- fail_wash: Detuned low wash as the performance fails
- grand_clock_chord: Layered clock-chord swell as the grand chime rings
- hall_ambience: Mechanical hall: gear rumble, faint steam hiss, distant audience murmur
